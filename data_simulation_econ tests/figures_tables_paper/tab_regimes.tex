\parbox{\linewidth}{\footnotesize Correlation regime of each pair from the sign concordance score S = 100 $\times$ (share of days on which both returns have the same sign $-$ share with opposite signs), computed on rolling windows (Win.) of 50 or 250 trading days, labelled with hysteresis: a regime starts when S reaches $\pm$15 and ends when S falls inside $\pm$5. Share = share of labelled days in each regime, which is the baseline of a rule whose hypothesis is that regime. Episodes = runs of consecutive days with the same label; Med.\ days = median episode length in trading days. Reversals = switches between a negative and a positive episode, possibly through a no-correlation spell; 2022--24 counts switches whose new episode starts in the test period. Samples (50-day windows): S\&P 500 -- Bond: 1971--2024; S\&P 500 -- Gold: 2000--2024; S\&P 500 -- Bitcoin: 2014--2024; Gold -- Bond: 2000--2024. Results with the deadband labels of the original rule ($\pm$10) are in the repository. Bond = 10-year zero-coupon Treasury (G\"urkaynak--Sack--Wright). Sample ends 31 Dec 2024.}
